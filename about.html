Our Story

A Logistics Partner You Can Rely On

Chuzzy Integrated Logistics Limited is a professional Nigerian logistics company with over 17 years of industry experience, delivering supply chain solutions to clients across the globe.

We understand that logistics is more than moving goods from one location to another. It is about protecting business continuity, meeting customer expectations, reducing operational friction and ensuring that every movement adds value to your supply chain.

Our approach combines operational excellence, responsive customer service and timely logistics solutions tailored to meet the unique logistics needs of our clients—with a focus on reliability, efficiency and customer satisfaction.

Our competence spans Air Freight, Sea Freight, Haulage, Clearing and forwarding and consultancy services, connecting businesses and delivering promises.
